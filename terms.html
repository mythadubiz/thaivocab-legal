2. Description of the App

ThaiVocab is a Thai-language vocabulary learning app. Its main features are:

  • A curated dictionary of approximately 4,000 Thai words across four difficulty tiers: Beginner, Survival, Conversational and Fluent
  • Translations into 16 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Hebrew, Indonesian, Malay and Arabic
  • Native-sounding audio narration for every word
  • Flashcard study mode with swipeable cards
  • Listening-based multiple-choice quiz with streak tracking
  • Progress tracking including per-word streaks and a quizzes-completed counter
  • Deck filters by tier and by topic category
  • Optional iCloud synchronization of preferences and progress
